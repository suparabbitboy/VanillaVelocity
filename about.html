Made by suparabbitboy

Vanilla Velocity is a Fabric 26.2 modpack created by suparabbitboy. It exists for players who want modern performance and a more comfortable Minecraft without leaving the vanilla experience behind.

Version 1.0 ships with a performance core, a large set of quality-of-life mods, six shader packs and twelve resource packs, chosen to work together so you can install once and just play.

This website was designed and written by suparabbitboy as the home of the pack.

How it optimizes your game

The foundation is Sodium for rendering, Lithium for game logic and FerriteCore for memory. Entity Culling and More Culling avoid drawing hidden objects, ImmediatelyFast speeds up the interface, and Dynamic FPS eases the load when the window is in the background.

Voxy extends view distance with level-of-detail rendering, Chunky pre-generates worlds, and Sodium Extra with Reese's Sodium Options give you tidy, detailed video settings. Iris loads shaders, with Complementary Reimagined as the easy pick for good looks at low cost.

Quality-of-life highlights

Xaero's Minimap and World Map handle navigation, while Jade and AppleSkin show what you are looking at and what food is worth eating. JEI with world generation info answers recipe and ore questions without leaving the game, and Mouse Tweaks and Shulker Box Tooltip make inventories less tedious.

Quick Pack, Resourcify and VT Downloader simplify resource packs, Screenshot Viewer and Screenshot Copy streamline sharing, Controlify adds controller support, and Sound Physics Remastered makes caves sound right. Each is small; together they change how the game feels.

FAQ

Can I add my own mods?

Yes. Add Fabric 26.2 compatible mods to your instance, but support is only offered for the pack as released.

Will it work on low-end PCs?

That is the goal. Skip shaders or choose a light pack like Complementary Reimagined, and lower render distance if needed.
